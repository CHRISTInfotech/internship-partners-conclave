INTERNSHIP PARTNERS’ CONCLAVE 2027

About the Conclave

The conclave brings CHRIST together with the employers, mentors and organisations who support students through internships. It is a chance to recognise that support, appreciate what partners contribute and connect around the next steps.

01

Recognise

Acknowledge the guidance partners give students.

02

Appreciate

Celebrate the people who make those opportunities possible.

03

Connect

Make room for conversation and future collaboration.

OUR CAMPUS

About CHRIST

At CHRIST’s Pune City Campus, students combine academic study with practical learning and industry engagement. The conclave brings internship partners to campus to recognise the role they play in that experience.
